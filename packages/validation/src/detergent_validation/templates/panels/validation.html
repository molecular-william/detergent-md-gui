Every row above is a constrained distance inside one ring, so the interaction that differs most between disaccharides — the bond, the four angles and the dihedral that span the two rings — is not in it at all. It is here, and it is where the same bead types stop agreeing. Each cell is the molecule's own atomistic mean with its coarse-grained mean after the arrow, and the beads it was measured over. A dihedral's published mean is a wrapped quantity, so its spread is withheld rather than shown as a number that invites a wrong reading.
