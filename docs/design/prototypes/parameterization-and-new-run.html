REVIEW

Decisions taken, and what to judge

Taken: the parameterization belongs to the mapping, and bonded states become its revisions (a run consumes a model, not a state id) · producing one is a job of this panel, not a run, because it makes no MD · three engines with the ensemble each saw named on the result · the AA reference is linked on the model and inherited by the run · the run panel's bonded step is read-only provenance · the checks are part of producing a fit and also run with a simulation.

Judge: (1) a model with no parameterization — the run fits it, or refuses and sends you here? (2) BARTENDER only -refit against our mapped trajectory, or its own GFN sampling too? (3) do the flat bonded-state list and a revision strip both survive, or does the strip replace the list? (4) is the job visible anywhere but this panel?

Nothing here is wired: this page makes no requests and touches no run.
