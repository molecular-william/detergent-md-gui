AA production output cadence

The NPT production stage editor gains five optional cadence boxes. A blank box keeps today's derivation: the trajectory stride follows the run-wide Trajectory interval (ps), and the log and energy intervals are that stride capped at 500 steps. An explicit trajectory step count overrides the ps interval for production only. Precision and COM removal are absent unless named, so an untouched run's MDP is unchanged.

Stage settings › NPT production

The five boxes sit under the stage's existing duration, timestep, neighbour list, Verlet tolerance and coupling controls, in a separate Output cadence block.
