New run · Nonbonded and constraints

One section inside Stage settings, shared by every dynamics leg of the run. AA: NVT, NPT and production. CG: equilibration and production. Minimisation keeps its reviewed values.

The numbers shown are the reviewed defaults the form will prefill. Blank means the reviewed profile or force-field value; for nsttcouple/nstpcouple and the AA LINCS fields a blank writes no line at all and leaves GROMACS' own rule in force (a Default click clears those).

Rejected combinations (named at the queue route and in the hint below): rlist below max(rcoulomb, rvdw); unequal rcoulomb/rvdw outside PME; vdwtype = PME with a switched modifier; a switched modifier without 0 < rvdw-switch < rvdw; epsilon-rf outside reaction-field or below epsilon-r; a cut-off that cannot fit the AA box.

AA run · aa_only

The AA half is PME, so the Coulomb type and the reaction-field permittivities are not offered. The force-field block's own cut-off values are the prefills.
