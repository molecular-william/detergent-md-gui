Surfaces: AA vs Simulated CG only, and no Align-trajectory tickbox

surfaces.generate measures two models: AA (the atomistic reference) and Simulated CG (this
run's own trajectory). The Mapped raw and Mapped ring-scaled variants are removed -- no mapped
trajectory is written and no mapped gmx sasa leg runs. One mapped value remains because the
comparison needs it: the first AA frame in the beads' frame is the rigid-body fit target that
registers the CG envelope onto AA (one frame of positions, not a surface calculation).

The run view's Shape section names two models and filters its table to AA and Simulated CG, so
an older attempt whose metadata still lists four variants reads the same way. The analysis-absent
sentence drops "the mapped".

The trajectory form's Align-trajectory checkbox is removed from markup, state, handler and help
text; Fit and Group are the only fit controls and are never disabled, and the default build
(no fit, no group) is unchanged. A recorded series restores the picks exactly as recorded.
