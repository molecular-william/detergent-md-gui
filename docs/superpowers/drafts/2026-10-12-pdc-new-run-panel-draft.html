A flat-area estimate from the structure: no curvature, no packing, no shape. It sizes an input and never decides that a micelle forms — the run's own assessment decides that. Illustrative numbers for this mock.

The first CG leg, and it is named for what happens to it: its end state is judged. Detergents not in the micelle are removed, and losing more than 10% of the requested count re-runs this leg from scratch with new velocities, up to three attempts. It is a leg that gets assessed, not a check beside one.
Continues the system that survived, from its checkpoint (gmx convert-tpr -extend), so the total CG sampling is the two legs together — 1 µs by default. The assessment re-runs at the end, and this is the state that gets back-mapped.

WORKFLOW

What this run will do

  • 1 · Prepare (optional)PDB2PQR for missing side chains and protonation, then energy minimisation in water. MemProtMD ships this as its own workflow; a structure that needs it is prepared once and reused.
  • 2 · Coarse-grainmartinize2, Martini 3, elastic network over Cα pairs within 1.0 nm at 1000 kJ/mol.
  • 3 · Self-assembleDetergents placed at random around the protein, then CG MD lets the belt find its own arrangement — MemProtMD's self-assembly route, rather than placing a pre-formed belt.
  • 4 · SampleCG MD: minimisation, then equilibration, then the assessment leg.
  • 5 · JudgeDetergents in the micelle are counted, the rest removed; losing more than 10% of the requested count re-runs the leg, up to three attempts.
  • 6 · Extend, then back-mapContinuation from the surviving checkpoint, then CG2AT2 with -o align.

With a protein, steps 3 and 5 use the protein's hydrophobic surface. For an empty micelle, step 3 is the micelle on its own and step 5's protein clause does not apply.
