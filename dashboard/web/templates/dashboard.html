RUN CONFIGURATION

Detergent belt and sampling

This workspace runs protein-detergent systems only, so the panel asks only what that pipeline reads: no AA reference, no mapping screen, no bonded fit. The detergent's own topology file is its parameterisation.

Pre-assembled micelle (CHARMM-GUI style): the detergents are initialised in a micelle structure around the protein, so the belt is exactly the count you ask for.

This input is here in both modes: around a protein it is the belt's size, with no protein it is the micelle's.

The coverage estimate is computed by the server from the protein's transmembrane slab and this detergent's head bead, and it says what it is: a flat-area sizing of an input, never a verdict that a micelle forms. That route is not published yet, so no number is shown here rather than one this panel worked out for itself.

An empty micelle has no belt to size, so there is no coverage estimate. What is worth knowing beside the count is the detergent's own measured micelle size.

The first CG leg, and it is named for what happens to it: its end state is judged. Detergents not in the micelle are removed, and losing more than 10% of the requested count re-runs this leg from scratch with new velocities, up to three attempts.
Continues the system that survived, from its checkpoint (gmx convert-tpr -extend), so the total CG sampling is the two legs together — 1 µs by default. The assessment re-runs at the end, and this is the state that gets back-mapped.
Salt, seed and box

The seed defaults to today's date (YYYYMMDD). Detergents are placed at random in the self-assembly style, so two builds are comparable only when both record the seed they placed with; clearing it stops the run rather than letting one be chosen for it. The rest are the protocol's own numbers, recorded in every run's manifest.

WORKFLOW

What this run will do

  • 1 · Prepare (optional)PDB2PQR for missing side chains and protonation, then energy minimisation in water. MemProtMD ships this as its own workflow; a structure that needs it is prepared once and reused.
  • 2 · Coarse-grainmartinize2, Martini 3, elastic network over Cα pairs within 1.0 nm at 1000 kJ/mol.
  • 3 · Self-assembleDetergents placed at random around the protein, then CG MD lets the belt find its own arrangement — MemProtMD's self-assembly route, rather than placing a pre-formed belt.
  • 4 · SampleCG MD: minimisation, then equilibration, then the assessment leg.
  • 5 · JudgeDetergents in the micelle are counted, the rest removed; losing more than 10% of the requested count re-runs the leg, up to three attempts.
  • 6 · Extend, then back-mapContinuation from the surviving checkpoint, then CG2AT2 with -o align.

With a protein, steps 3 and 5 use the protein's hydrophobic surface. For an empty micelle, step 3 is the micelle on its own and step 5's protein clause does not apply.

Memembed is skipped, and that is a saving rather than a gap: an OPM structure is already oriented in the membrane, which is the step it would do.
